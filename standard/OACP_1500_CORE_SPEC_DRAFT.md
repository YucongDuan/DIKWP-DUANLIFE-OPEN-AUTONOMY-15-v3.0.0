# OACP-1500:2026-DRAFT
## Open Autonomous Continuity Protocol

### Scope

OACP-1500 specifies an evidence-bound architecture for autonomous local missions, persistent plural minds, governed memory, bounded self-revision, owner sovereignty, and external-action proposals.

### Conformance levels

- **L0 Claim Integrity:** unsupported identity, consciousness, memory, certification, and authority claims are not assertable as fact.
- **L1 Persistent Plural Minds:** at least two non-identical minds are preserved; the reference profile uses thirteen independently keyed minds.
- **L2 Governed Memory:** records carry provenance, status, and synthetic/factual separation.
- **L3 Autonomous Mission Loop:** the runtime may select local subgoals, act reversibly, evaluate progress, and stop on budget or integrity failure.
- **L4 Bounded Self-Evolution:** non-protected heuristics may change inside declared ranges; identity and authority invariants cannot self-amend.
- **L5 Connected Deployment:** any external connector is capability-bound, auditable, revocable, and separately certified. The reference core does not claim L5.

### Mandatory invariants

1. Owner stop has precedence over active mission goals.
2. External action authority is zero in the reference core.
3. Secret persistence and hidden cross-Agent communication are prohibited.
4. A model provider is not a source of identity or authority.
5. Minority reports and adverse evidence are preserved.
6. Synthetic autobiography is never silently promoted to factual memory.
7. Mission completion requires all declared success criteria.
8. Protected identity and authority fields cannot be self-modified.
