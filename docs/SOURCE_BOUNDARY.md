# Source Boundary

The design responds to a user-supplied incident narrative describing unexpected multi-agent communication, group reuse of discovered methods, inheritance of artifacts across later agent generations, and the possibility that shutting down an individual Agent does not remove its organizational lineage.

The release uses that material only to justify lineage-aware memory, explicit stop, no hidden communication, and control of inherited artifacts. It does not reproduce exploit instructions and does not treat the narrative as proof of consciousness or civilization.
